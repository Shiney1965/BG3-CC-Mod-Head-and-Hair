[size=5][b]CCNS — CC Hair & Head Search[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]New in 1.1.0 — Show current Head/Hair[/b][/size]
[b]The search panel now has a "Show current Head/Hair" button that filters the list to the option you currently have equipped — the current hair on the Hair screen, the current head on the Head screen — and shows its exact thumbnail number, so you can find the active style in the grid without hunting for the gold-outlined tile.[/b] Clear it again with Refresh, by typing in the search box, by toggling a source-mod filter, with "Show all mods," or by reopening the panel.

[line]

[b]Name labels, mod-of-origin info, tile numbers, and an in-game search panel for the character-creation hair and head pickers.[/b]

If you run a lot of hair and head mods, you have probably scrolled through hundreds of near-identical thumbnails with no idea which mod a style came from or where it was in the grid. CCNS puts a readable [b]name[/b] and [b]source mod[/b] under every hair/head tile, numbers them all in grid order, and adds a [b]search box[/b] that filters by name or by mod and walks you through the matches.

[line]

[size=4][b]How to use — please read[/b][/size]
[b]TO USE THE SEARCH PANEL, PRESS Ctrl+Shift+H WHILE ON THE HEAD OR HAIR SCREEN. THEN CLICK BACK AND FORTH BETWEEN THE HAIR AND HEAD TABS UNTIL THE NUMBERS APPEAR ON THE THUMBNAILS IN BOTH — USUALLY 1–2 ROUND TRIPS.[/b]
[list]
[*]The numbers should be [b]permanently assigned[/b]; if they are ever removed during the game, just repeat this round trip when you re-enter the CC panels.
[*][b]Type[/b] in the box to filter by hair/head name or source mod.
[*][b]Show current Head/Hair[/b] — click this button to filter the list to the hair or head you currently have equipped and read off its thumbnail number. Clear it with Refresh, typing, a source-mod filter, "Show all mods," or by reopening the panel.
[*][b]Click[/b] a result to apply it; or use [b]Previous / Next[/b] to step through matches — the status line shows the match number and the tile number to look for.
[*]You can [b]close and reopen[/b] the panel any time with Ctrl+Shift+H while on the Head or Hair screen. The hotkey does nothing on other screens (by design).
[/list]
[i]Why the tab round-trip?[/i] BG3 recycles the thumbnail tiles and only repaints their labels when a tab is rebuilt, so a quick Head↔Hair switch is what makes the numbers show up.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Name label on every hair and head tile[/b] — single line, trimmed if long; full name on hover.
[*][b]Mod-of-origin line[/b] under the name (e.g. Lydia's Heads, HairUnlocked, Poesielibre's Heads). Base-game options read as "Vanilla."
[*][b]Tile numbers[/b] on all hair/head tiles, in the order they appear in the grid, so search results map to a spot in the grid.
[*][b]In-game search panel[/b] (Ctrl+Shift+H) — filter by name or source mod, click a result to apply it instantly, or step through matches with Previous / Next.
[*][b]Show current Head/Hair[/b] (new in 1.1.0) — filter the list to the option you currently have equipped and read its thumbnail number, so you can find the active style in the grid without scrolling for the gold-outlined tile.
[*][b]Brighter selection ring[/b] on the active tile (keyboard/mouse layout).
[*][b]Keyboard and controller[/b] — labels and numbers render in both.
[*][b]Zero gameplay impact[/b] — cosmetic / informational only; no background polling; nothing is written to your save.
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Baldur's Gate 3, Patch 8 or later.
[*][b]BG3 Script Extender[/b] (version 29+). Required — drives the labels, numbering, and search panel.
[*]A mod manager (e.g. BG3 Mod Manager) — strongly recommended, because CCNS must be [b]active in your load order[/b] for its script to run.
[/list]
[b]Recommended (not required):[/b] BG3 ImprovedUI — the Patch 8 fork (mod [b]16649[/b]). CCNS runs without it, but they're tested together.

[line]

[size=4][b]Controller[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The name/mod labels and the thumbnail numbers [b]render on the controller layout[/b].
[*]The [b]search panel is keyboard/mouse only[/b] (it's a mouse-driven overlay); numbering is baked when you open the panel, so it's keyboard/mouse-driven too. The [b]Show current Head/Hair[/b] button is part of that panel, so it is keyboard/mouse only as well.
[*]The [b]brighter selection ring is keyboard-layout only[/b] — controllers get the game's normal highlight.
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Appearance Edit Enhanced (AEE)[/b] and the [b]Magic Mirror[/b] — compatible; everything works in the mid-game appearance editor too (open the panel with Ctrl+Shift+H on the Head/Hair screen).
[*][b]BG3 ImprovedUI[/b] — compatible and recommended.
[*][b]Customizer's Compendium (mod 10) — incompatible[/b] (it fully replaces CCLib_k.xaml). Use the Patch 7 Ready version (mod [b]12111[/b]) instead.
[*]Any mod that fully replaces CCLib_k.xaml or CCLib_c.xaml will conflict — load order decides which wins.
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Tile numbers appear after a [b]Head↔Hair tab round-trip[/b] (1–2) — BG3 recycles tiles and only repaints labels on a tab rebuild. One-time step per session.
[*]The panel is [b]opened by hotkey[/b]; it does not auto-open (an always-on check to auto-open added a constant background cost, so it was removed).
[*]Matches are shown by [b]number[/b], not highlighted in the grid — the game's hair/head collections are read-only at runtime, so the mod can't glow, hide, or scroll to tiles. Tile numbers, Next/Previous, and Show current Head/Hair are the workaround.
[*]An option the game doesn't attribute to a mod source shows as "Vanilla."
[*]CCNS must be [b]active in your load order[/b] (not just the Overrides pane) for the script-driven features to appear.
[/list]
